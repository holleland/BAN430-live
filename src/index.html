About the numbers. The forecast is the seasonal naive benchmark from the assignment page: each day is predicted to cost what the same weekday cost last week, with uncertainty from 1000 simulated paths that resample the model's past errors. The projected October average is the average of the known days plus the simulated rest of the month. Your task is to beat it.
Prices: NO5 hourly spot prices excluding VAT, averaged per day, from hvakosterstrommen.no. Tomorrow's prices appear at about 13:00.
